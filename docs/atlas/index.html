    About the Atlas

    The Atlas covers construction- and arbitration-related decisions of Ugandan courts and tribunals published on the Uganda Legal Information Institute (ULII) from 2015 to 2026: the Supreme Court, the Court of Appeal, all divisions of the High Court, the PPDA Appeals Tribunal, CADER and other tribunals. It includes decisions in which construction parties face related risks, such as tax, banking and insolvency disputes.

    Each decision is classified by stream (construction; construction disputes that reached court through an arbitration application; other arbitration), type of proceeding, the dispute issues in play, the outcome for the party that brought the proceeding, and, for arbitration matters, the application type and grounds raised. Citations of legislation and of other decisions in the archive are extracted from the judgments.

    How the data were made

    Decisions were collected from ULII registers and full-text judgments, screened for relevance, and de-duplicated, with every exclusion logged. Classifications are produced by transparent, rule-based coding (version 1), validated field by field against a 150-decision reference set that is currently AI-coded and is being verified by hand. Agreement is high for stream, court, proceeding and state involvement, moderate for outcome, and lower for some issues; treat classifications as a research guide and always read the judgment.

    What the Atlas does not do

    • It does not host judgments. Every case links to the full text on ULII, which remains the authoritative source.
    • It does not reproduce ULII's summaries or headnotes.
    • It does not publish amounts claimed or awarded case by case until they have been verified.
    • It is not legal advice.

    Digests

    Short digests of leading decisions, written by the author from the judgments, will be added progressively alongside the forthcoming book Construction and Arbitration Law in Uganda: Cases, Evidence and Reform, 2015–2026.
