      About the Atlas

      The Atlas is the first systematic, searchable record of how Uganda's courts and tribunals decide construction and arbitration disputes. It covers decisions published on the Uganda Legal Information Institute (ULII) from 2015 to 2026: the Supreme Court, the Court of Appeal, all divisions of the High Court, the PPDA Appeals Tribunal, CADER and other tribunals, including disputes in which construction parties face related risks such as tax, banking and insolvency.

      Decisions are divided into two archives. Construction cases are construction disputes, including those that reached court through an arbitration application. Arbitration cases are applications under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, including those arising from construction contracts. Decisions in the overlap appear in both, marked "via arbitration".

      How the data were made

      Decisions were collected from ULII registers and full-text judgments, screened and de-duplicated, with every exclusion logged. Classifications come from transparent, rule-based coding (version 1), validated field by field against a 150-decision reference set that is currently AI-coded and being verified by hand. Agreement is high for stream, court, proceeding and state involvement, moderate for outcome, and lower for some issues. Subsequent treatment is rule-based: citations are classified from the words around each mention, and decisions with the same parties are grouped into matters. Summaries come from the author's case register and are paraphrased; some are derived from ULII's summaries.

      What the Atlas does not do

      • It does not host judgments: every case links to the full text on ULII, the authoritative source.
      • It does not publish amounts claimed or awarded case by case until they are verified.
      • It is not legal advice. Always read the judgment.
